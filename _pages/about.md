I completed my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at [Worcester Polytechnic Institute](https://www.wpi.edu/) in May 2026, where I did research in the InfoLab with [Prof. Kyumin Lee](https://web.cs.wpi.edu/~kmlee/). Before that, I earned my B.Tech in AI & Machine Learning from Anurag University. I am applying to PhD programs for [Fall 2027].

I'm broadly interested in building **reliable deep learning models**, with a focus on **computer vision and multimodal learning**. More specifically, I am interested in:

- Out-of-distribution generalization in vision and vision-language models.
- Benchmark and dataset design that shows the gap between benchmark scores and real-world reliability.
- Evaluation methodology, including combining automatic metrics with human judgment.

My main project, **Captive or Wild?**, is a multimodal benchmark classifying whether wildlife in an image is captive or wild, across nine CITES-protected species. Our model, WildSIFE, uses structured BLIP-2 captions and adversarial species-invariant training so it learns cues about the environment instead of species shortcuts. It holds up on species it never saw in training and outperforms six baselines, including zero-shot CLIP, BioCLIP and DINOv2 by 5.2% .
